# Local Blender Toyota 4Runner N185 reconstruction — 17 November 2026

## Scope and result

The owner rejected the first procedurally detailed SUV because its body did not
resemble the N185, and requested internet references and blueprint-based rebuilding.
The old draft is preserved; a new editable body was constructed in a separate scene.
This is a reference-constrained visual reconstruction, not original Toyota CAD,
a scan, an engineering model, or an owner-accepted exact replica.

No Family Connect implementation, version, client/server installation, deployment,
catalog, policy or release gate changed. Existing dirty repository work is preserved.
Blender5.3.0 and MCP for Blender2.3.0 remain unchanged; local safe mode remains enabled.

## Reference evidence

- [Toyota USA 2001 brochure](https://www.auto-brochures.com/makes/Toyota/4Runner/Toyota_US%204Runner_2001.pdf): dimensions, US equipment and suspension layout.
- [Toyota 1999 Hilux Surf catalogue scan](https://www.japanclassic.ru/en/booklets/438-toyota-hilux-surf-1999-rzn180-kzn185.html): specification-page side/top dimension drawings, N185 body profile, pillars and plan taper.
- [Toyota corporate N185 history/specifications](https://www.toyota-global.com/company/history_of_toyota/75years/vehicle_lineage/car/id60011420/): generation identity,2675mm wheelbase and1800mm wide-body specification.
- [Toyota Canada press photographs](https://media.toyota.ca/en/albums/1997/1997-toyota-4runner.html): shared-generation body curvature and glazing. The pre-facelift grille/bumper are not used as the facelift master.
- [ImportArchive profile](https://importarchive.com/toyota/4runner/1996-2002): secondary visual reference only; its stylized length/wheelbase ratio does not match factory dimensions.
- Public collision-sheet samples were inspected but are too small for reliable hard-point measurements. No paid drawing/model was purchased or access restriction bypassed.
- The owner's photo controls silver finish, US facelift appearance, aftermarket stance, black wheels and basket. Optical lens detail is projected from that photo and includes source reflections.

The JDM drawing includes an external spare wheel that is not present on the US
reference. US bumper length and fascia are treated separately. The whole-page scan
is only800 pixels wide; tracing does not establish millimetre-accurate body surfaces.
No claim is made to have collected every internet reference or found full OEM CAD.

## Validation and limits

The local MCP code guard validates the submitted scripts. Geometry readback confirms
wheel centres X=±1.3375m, wheelbase2.674999982m, bumper envelope length4.654999971m,
bumper envelope width1.805920m and no non-finite mesh vertices. These measurements
check selected anchors, not whole-body similarity or manufacturing tolerances.

EEVEE renders are generated and visually inspected rather than inferred from script
success. Surface-normal/thickness direction and glazing/header closure are corrected
during inspection. The source photos and drawings retain their owners' rights;
no redistribution license is asserted. No reference image or model is pushed to Git.

Remaining: owner likeness acceptance; finer surface reconstruction from higher-quality
orthographic data if exact matching is required. Lift/offset/basket dimensions are
estimated; interior and undercarriage are simplified; panels are not a certified
watertight printable shell. No animation, simulation or engineering acceptance.
